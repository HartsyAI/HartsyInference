# Whisper small.en per-utterance latency on the RTX 3060 — 2026-09-30

Bring-up perf pass for the phone-call voice-agent plan's STT gate: `openai/whisper-small.en`, in-process and warm on
the 3060, per utterance ≤ 350 ms for 2, 5 and 10 s of 16 kHz speech; narrowband (8 k → 16 k) content-word recall ≥
the 16 kHz baseline − 10 points; the JFK clip keeps 11/11 words. Branch `perf/whisper-voice-latency` (PR #204).
Everything below is in-process (`tests/HartsyInference.Audio.Tests/WhisperBenchTests.cs`), real weights, model load
excluded.

## Remaining cost and levers (not taken)

At 10 s the 200 ms splits into decode ~120 ms (host-issue bound), encoder ~52 ms (TF32 GEMMs and the materialized
attention over 1499 positions), mel ~17 ms (single-threaded host), and cross K/V and prompt ~11 ms. A captured decode
step (one graph launch per token over the fixed-capacity cache) would remove most of the decode's host time, but it
needs the self-attention on `FlashAttention` over the fixed buffer instead of the masked SDPA over a copied prefix: a
numerics change, not needed for the gate. Frame-parallel mel through `CpuParallel.For` is output-identical and worth
~10 ms at 10 s. The encoder window stays the full 30 s: no shortened window, no change to the model's input.

The front-end still differs from OpenAI's `log_mel_spectrogram` in two pre-existing ways this pass left alone because
changing them changes the output: a 512-point FFT over the 400-sample window (257 bins; the reference uses a 400-point
STFT, 201 bins), and no centering (2997 frames → 1499 encoder positions against the reference's 3000 → 1500).
